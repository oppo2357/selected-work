Design notes & photography

Visual direction

Warm paper and dark pine frame two photographs. Large Instrument Serif headlines take their cue from a printed travel journal. DM Sans keeps navigation and trip details easy to scan. The two-column cover becomes a single reading column on small screens.

Paper #FAF8F1 · Pine #203B32 · Berry #742F47
