Room key pickup notice

An independently written, one-page notice for a fictional community centre. A reader needs to find the right pickup desk and entrance for a given booking time.

Reading the schedule

Both files were exported from the same editable source. In the tagged file, one main heading and three section headings let readers navigate the notice. The three column headings identify the booking time, pickup desk and entrance. The nine data cells follow the printed schedule row by row.
